About me

Analytics that the business can rely on

I work across the analytics space, from data models and dashboards to the automations that take manual work off people's plates. Most of my experience is in finance, building reporting that teams rely on to close the month, where accuracy and deadlines matter.

I'm comfortable owning the systems behind the numbers, not just reporting on them. I understand how ERP systems support the business, and when something needs to change I take it from the business requirement through to testing and go-live, working with IT teams and vendors along the way. Bridging business users and technical teams is the part of the work I enjoy most.

I like building solutions that connect: data models that power both reporting and automation, and AI agents that handle recurring work. My portfolio projects on this site apply the same approach end to end, from raw data to a finished product.

Dubai Property Market & Mortgage Risk

How a UAE bank's risk team could value homes, track prices and stress-test its mortgage book, built from Dubai Land Department open data. An end-to-end pipeline from raw files to a published Power BI report, with a like-for-like price index, a valuation model and a stress test.
